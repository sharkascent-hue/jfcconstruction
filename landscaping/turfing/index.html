FAQs

Your questions.

Do you prepare the ground before laying a patio or lawn?

Always. Levels, drainage and a compacted base go in first, which is what stops patios sinking and lawns waterlogging.

Can you do the whole garden in one project?

Yes. Patios, paths, walls, fencing, decking and lawns can all be planned and built together by one team.

Do you take away the waste?

Yes. Garden waste, old slabs and spoil are removed and the site is left clean.

How do I get a price?

Call or WhatsApp us a few photos of the garden and we’ll arrange a free site visit and a no-obligation quote.
